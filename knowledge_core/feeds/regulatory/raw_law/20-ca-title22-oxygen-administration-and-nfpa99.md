## Text

> "The licensee shall be permitted to accept or retain a resident who requires the use of oxygen administration, under the following circumstances, requiring either independent self-administration or supervision by an appropriately skilled professional, documented fire-safety signage reading No Smoking-Oxygen in Use, electrical safety checks, secured tanks, and documentation of care in the resident's file."
