## §255.0 — Definitions

**Endorsement:**
> "Any advertising, marketing, or promotional message for a product that consumers are likely to
> believe reflects the opinions, beliefs, findings, or experiences of a party other than the
> sponsoring advertiser."

**Product:**
> "Includes any product, service, brand, company, or industry."

**Expert:**
> "An individual, group, or institution possessing, as a result of experience, study, or training,
> knowledge of a particular subject, which knowledge is superior to what ordinary individuals
> generally acquire."

**Clear and Conspicuous:**
> "A disclosure is difficult to miss (i.e., easily noticeable) and easily understandable by ordinary
> consumers."

## §255.1 — General Considerations

**(a):**
> "Endorsements must reflect the honest opinions, findings, beliefs, or experience of the endorser."

**(b):**
> "An endorser may be liable for statements made in the course of their endorsements, such as when an
> endorser makes a representation that the endorser knows or should know to be deceptive."

**(c):**
> "When the advertisement represents that the endorser uses the endorsed product, the endorser must
> have been a bona fide user of it at the time the endorsement was given."

**(d):**
> "Advertisers are subject to liability for misleading or unsubstantiated statements made through
> endorsements or for failing to disclose unexpected material connections."

**(g):**
> "The use of an endorsement with the image or likeness of a person other than the actual endorser is
> deceptive if it misrepresents a material attribute of the endorser."
