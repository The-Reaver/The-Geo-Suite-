## Text (as surfaced by search, attributed to the FTC)

Key points reported from the guidance:
- AI marketing tools "must work as advertised."
- "Artificial intelligence" is characterized as "a marketing term" that marketers must not overuse or
  abuse — i.e., calling something "AI-powered" when it is not, or overstating what an AI feature
  actually does, is treated as a potential §5 deception issue.
- Published by the FTC's Division of Advertising Practices staff, February 27, 2023.
