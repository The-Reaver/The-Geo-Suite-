# NIST AI Risk Management Framework (AI RMF 1.0)

- **Jurisdiction:** Federal
- **Domain:** AI-visibility / AI-generated content honesty
- **Source:** https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework
- **Retrieved:** 2026-08-15
- **Retrieval method:** Direct fetch, verbatim
- **Verification status:** Not yet lawyer-reviewed
- **Note on legal weight:** Explicitly voluntary, non-binding — see the quoted scope language below.
  This is the closest thing to an authoritative federal AI-governance reference point that currently
  exists, but it is not law. Label accordingly in any atomic note: reference-framework weight, not
  statute-weight.

## Text

**Stated purpose:**
> "to better manage risks to individuals, organizations, and society associated with artificial
> intelligence (AI)"

**Stated scope (voluntary status):**
> "intended for voluntary use and to improve the ability to incorporate trustworthiness considerations
> into the design, development, use, and evaluation of AI products, services, and systems"

## Why this is here

A reference framework for what "responsible AI" governance looks like in the absence of binding
federal AI legislation — useful context for the AI-visibility domain even though it creates no
enforceable obligation on its own. FTC Act §5 (file 01) and the AI-claims guidance (file 10) remain
the actual enforcement hooks; this file is background, not a rule to cite as binding.
