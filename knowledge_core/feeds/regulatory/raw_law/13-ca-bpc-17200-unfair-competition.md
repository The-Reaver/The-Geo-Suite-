# Cal. Business & Professions Code §17200 — Unfair Competition Law

- **Jurisdiction:** California
- **Domain:** Medical marketing / device claims
- **Source:** https://leginfo.legislature.ca.gov/faces/codes_displaySection.xhtml?lawCode=BPC&sectionNum=17200
- **Retrieved:** 2026-08-15
- **Retrieval method:** Direct fetch, verbatim (excerpt)
- **Verification status:** Not yet lawyer-reviewed

## Text

> "unfair competition shall mean and include any unlawful, unfair or fraudulent business act or
> practice and unfair, deceptive, untrue or misleading advertising"

Also expressly incorporates §17500 by reference:
> "any act prohibited by Chapter 1 (commencing with Section 17500) of Part 3 of Division 7 of the
> Business and Professions Code."

Last amended: Stats. 1992, Ch. 430, Sec. 2, effective January 1, 1993.

## Why this is here

California's broadest civil-enforcement hook — the UCL's "unlawful" prong means a violation of almost
any other law (including federal FTC Act §5, or the false-advertising statute in file 12) can also be
pursued as a §17200 claim, with its own private-right-of-action exposure distinct from FTC enforcement.
