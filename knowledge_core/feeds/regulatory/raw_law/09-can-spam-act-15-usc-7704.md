# CAN-SPAM Act — 15 U.S.C. §7704(a)

- **Jurisdiction:** Federal
- **Domain:** Patient data privacy / lead-contact compliance
- **Source:** https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/15/7704
- **Retrieved:** 2026-08-15
- **Retrieval method:** Direct fetch, verbatim
- **Verification status:** Not yet lawyer-reviewed

## Text

**(1) Prohibition of false or misleading transmission information:**
> "It is unlawful for any person to initiate the transmission, to a protected computer, of a
> commercial electronic mail message, or a transactional or relationship message, that contains, or is
> accompanied by, header information that is materially false or materially misleading."

**(2) Prohibition of deceptive subject headings:**
> "It is unlawful for any person to initiate the transmission to a protected computer of a commercial
> electronic mail message if such person has actual knowledge...that a subject heading of the message
> would be likely to mislead a recipient."

**(3) Inclusion of return address or comparable mechanism:** requires commercial email to contain "a
functioning return electronic mail address or other Internet-based mechanism, clearly and
conspicuously displayed" allowing recipients to opt-out within 30 days.

**(4) Prohibition of transmission after objection:** unlawful to send commercial email within 10
business days after receiving an opt-out request, or to transfer recipient addresses to third parties.

**(5) Inclusion of identifier, opt-out, and physical address:** requires clear identification as
advertisement, notice of opt-out opportunity, and valid sender address.
