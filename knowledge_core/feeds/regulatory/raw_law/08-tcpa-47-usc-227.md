# TCPA — 47 U.S.C. §227(b)(1)

- **Jurisdiction:** Federal
- **Domain:** Patient data privacy / lead-contact compliance — becomes directly relevant once the
  platform's Twilio-dependent SMS features start contacting real leads generated from these audits
- **Source:** https://www.law.cornell.edu/uscode/text/47/227
- **Retrieved:** 2026-08-15
- **Retrieval method:** Direct fetch, verbatim
- **Verification status:** Not yet lawyer-reviewed

## Text

It shall be unlawful for any person to:

**(A):**
> "make any call (other than a call made for emergency purposes or made with the prior express consent
> of the called party) using any automatic telephone dialing system or an artificial or prerecorded
> voice" to emergency lines, hospital rooms, or cellular/paging services where the recipient is
> charged, unless collecting a federal debt.

**(B):**
> "initiate any telephone call to any residential telephone line using an artificial or prerecorded
> voice to deliver a message without the prior express consent of the called party," with exceptions
> for emergencies, federal debt collection, or Commission exemptions.

**(C):** Use fax machines or devices to send unsolicited advertisements to fax machines, subject to
specific conditions regarding established business relationships and notice requirements.

**(D):**
> "use any automatic telephone dialing system in such a way that two or more telephone lines of a
> multi-line business are engaged simultaneously."
