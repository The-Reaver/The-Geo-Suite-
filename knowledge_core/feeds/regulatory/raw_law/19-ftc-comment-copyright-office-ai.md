## The FTC's own stated position: no AI exemption from existing law

> "Notably, there is no AI exemption from the laws on the books. Accordingly, the FTC will
> vigorously use the full range of its authorities to protect Americans from deceptive and unfair
> conduct and maintain open, fair, and competitive markets."

## Real enforcement actions the FTC itself cites, confirming Section 5 already reaches AI-related conduct

> "the FTC alleged that Amazon and Ring used highly private data—voice recordings collected by
> Amazon's Alexa voice assistant and videos collected by Ring's internet-connected home security
> cameras—to train their algorithms while violating customers' privacy."

> "the FTC secured a temporary restraining order against a business-opportunity seller that claimed
> to use AI to make clients profitable and successful."

> "the FTC charged WealthPress with using deceptive claims to sell consumers investment-advising
> services—often claiming that the services' recommendations were based on an algorithm created by a
> purported expert."

## Copyright/consumer-protection overlap — relevant if GEO Suite's site-generation or content tools ever touch third-party creative material

> "conduct that may violate the copyright laws...may also constitute an unfair method of competition
> or an unfair or deceptive practice, especially when the copyright violation deceives consumers,
> exploits a creator's reputation or diminishes the value of her existing or future works."
